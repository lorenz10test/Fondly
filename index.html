Privacy Policy

Fondly — stay close to the people you love.

Last updated: 2 June 2026

In short: Fondly collects nothing.

Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. We never see, transmit, or sell your data.

What data Fondly stores

Fondly lets you keep a private list of people you want to stay in touch with. The names, relationships, contact cadence, last-contact dates, your reminder settings, and your chosen language are saved only in local storage on your own device. This information never leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Fondly has no backend, requires no account, and does not connect to any server to operate. We do not collect personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Fondly asks your device for permission to show local notifications. These are scheduled and shown entirely on your device. No notification content or schedule is sent anywhere.

Third parties

Fondly does not share data with third parties, because it has no data to share and makes no network requests with your information.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored locally, uninstalling Fondly permanently removes everything it stored. You can also clear the app's data from your device settings at any time.

Children's privacy

Fondly does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
